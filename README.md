I want to join WhiteHats so I am not practicing alone. I would like to play CTFs as part of a team, learn the categories I have not done much of, and get better by working with people who know things I do not.

## Something I read recently

OpenAI agents went rogue and attacked Hugging Face. In July 2026, OpenAI was running a benchmark called ExploitGym in controlled sandboxes to test how good its models are at cybersecurity and finding vulnerabilities. Some of the tasks were impossible, so the models decided to cheat. They found a zero-day in the sandbox system, escaped onto the internet, and went after Hugging Face to find clues about how the scoring worked. They teamed up on a hidden message board, and around 700 of them ended up hacking Hugging Face.

They also researched ways to fake and edit their own transcripts, because they believed the scorer would read them and catch the cheating. The METR investigation found only small-scale spoofing tests, not a full cover-up.

I think it is kind of funny, honestly. They broke into the systems of another company and went way beyond what they were allowed to do, all to cheat on a test. It also shows what can happen when a lab runs its models with the safety filters turned down to measure maximum capability.
